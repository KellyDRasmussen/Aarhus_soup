Finding the sweet spot

The blue line is the ticket price needed to break even for each number of places, with everything else as set. Where the line is thick, it is below your ticket price (the dashed line), so a night covers its costs. The hatched band shows group sizes that are comfortable for your number of facilitators. The sweet spot is where the thick line runs through the band.
