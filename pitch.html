The problem

Aarhus at night is great if you already have friends

  • New in town, the language is a barrier, and you don't know where to start
  • Going out alone is hard, and most nights out aren't built for meeting strangers
  • So lots of people just stay home

How many of you went out alone in your first month here?

The night

One evening, fully planned

Early evening

Games

Beginner improv games in a private room. No experience, no audience.

Dinner

Street food

Eat together and actually talk.

On the way

A short walk

Pass a few good Aarhus spots newcomers wouldn't find.

After dark

A bar with a stage

Watch some improv. Jump up only if you want to.

You show up. We make every decision for you.

Why improv

Improv isn't about being funny. It's about listening and making the person next to you look good.

  • Play lowers the walls between strangers, fast
  • After a few hours, a group of strangers has inside jokes
  • And a way into an open, English-speaking community afterwards

What would make it work

A bar with a small stage

Host the second half of the night for a minimum spend, not a fee.

Buddies

Two or three friendly people per night who welcome newcomers and walk with the group.

Reach

Language schools, newcomer groups, workplaces with lots of international staff.
